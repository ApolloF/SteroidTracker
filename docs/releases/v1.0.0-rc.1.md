**Release candidate for SteroidTracker 1.0.** Track TRT, HRT, peptides and bloodwork. Offline.

Install **SteroidTracker-v1.0.0-rc.1.apk** over 0.6.0 or 0.5.x; it updates in place and your plan, logs, journal and settings stay. This is a pre-release: please report anything that looks wrong before 1.0.0.

## Today
- **Check time:** the clock chip in the header (left of the calendar button) shows "Now". Tap it to pick a time; while it is set, the round check, "Log all", "Log missed as taken" and checks on a picked past day record that time, and the snackbar says "… taken at 8:00". It goes back to "Now" with the dialog's Now button, when you leave Today or the app, or when the day changes. The Log dose, blood pressure, note and symptom sheets keep their own Now/Earlier choice.
- If updating the presets or log times fails at start, Today shows "Startup update failed" with what was not done, instead of the app closing.

## Levels
- The Logged + plan / Logged / Plan only choice is now on the overview too, and it is one setting for the whole tab (it also rides in backups).
- **Level model corrections** (sources in `docs/MODELS.md`):
  - Long esters no longer give more hormone exposure per mg than the short ones. Per mg of hormone, Tren E and Parabolan now match Tren A, Masteron E matches Masteron P, and Test PP, Isocaproate and Decanoate match the mean of Test E, C and P. Their estimated levels are lower than in 0.6.0, for example Tren E 100 mg every 3.5 days averages about 630 ng/dL instead of 4,600.
  - Telmisartan uses the 40 mg study arm (peak 163 ng/mL at 40 mg instead of 308).
  - Your logged amounts and times are not changed; past doses of these compounds are redrawn with the corrected model. If you set Adjust level on Trenbolone or Drostanolone to bring the old curve down, it now applies on top of the lower curve; check it.

## Other changes
- Settings › Privacy policy shows the privacy policy inside the app.
- The app no longer declares the network-state permission (WorkManager added it; nothing used it).
- A separate Google Play build exists; this GitHub APK is unchanged by it: all presets, everything unlocked, no internet permission.

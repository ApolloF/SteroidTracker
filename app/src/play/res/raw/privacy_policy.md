# Privacy policy (Google Play version)

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy covers the app with the package name com.apollof.protocoltracker as installed from Google Play. It is an Android app for logging doses, blood pressure, symptoms and lab results. Everything you log is stored on your phone. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and the app never sends what you log to ApolloF or anyone else. The only connection the app makes is to Google Play, for the optional Pro purchase (see "Pro purchase").

## Who is responsible

The app is made and published by ApolloF, in the Netherlands. Contact: me@apollof.nl. Because the app never sends your data to ApolloF, ApolloF doesn't hold or process it; it stays on your phone and in files you export yourself. If you email ApolloF, ApolloF is the controller of that email (see "Your rights").

## What you log stays on your phone

- Your plans, doses, journal entries, blood pressure, symptoms, lab results and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it. The app doesn't add its own encryption, so the data is protected by your phone's screen lock and Android's device encryption.
- Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this data, so it isn't copied to Google or to a new phone, and the app doesn't allow Android backups through other tools. Use the app's own backup file to move it.
- Reminders and the home-screen widget are handled on the phone. Reminder notifications and the widget show the names and doses of your scheduled items, so they can be visible on the lock screen or home screen depending on your Android notification and lock-screen settings.

## Pro purchase

- Pro is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play's billing system. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); the app never sees your payment details.
- To check whether you own Pro, the app asks the Google Play Store app on your phone. This request names the app and the Pro product only and contains nothing you logged.
- The app stores on your phone whether Pro is unlocked and when you first opened a feature with a free trial. The trial dates are included in your backup file.
- Google gives ApolloF the sales reports every Play developer receives (for example the order number, date and price, and the buyer's country, region and postal code). ApolloF uses them only for accounting and tax, keeps them as long as Dutch tax law requires, and cannot link them to anything you log.

## Permissions

- **Notifications, exact alarms and vibration:** for dose reminders at the times you set.
- **Run at startup:** to set your reminders again after the phone restarts or the app updates.
- **Keep awake and foreground service:** added by WorkManager, the Android library the app uses to re-check reminders in the background about every 12 hours.
- **Internet, view network connections and Google Play billing:** added by the Google Play Billing Library, for the Pro purchase only.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permission.

## Files you export

The JSON backup (which includes your app settings) and the reports (HTML, and Markdown for AI tools) are saved only where you choose in Android's file picker. Backup and import files are read only from the file you pick. These files are **not encrypted** and contain your health data, so keep them somewhere private. If you save them to a cloud folder, that service's privacy terms apply.

## Chatbot lab import and reports for AI tools

To import lab results, the app gives you a prompt to copy into a chatbot of your choice together with your lab report, and you paste the answer back. The prompt contains none of your data. The app copies it to Android's clipboard when you tap *Copy AI prompt* and reads the clipboard only when you tap *Paste answer*; other apps may be able to read the clipboard while it holds that text. The app itself sends nothing: whatever you paste into a chatbot, or upload a report to, goes to that service under its own privacy terms. Nothing is saved in the app before you tap Save.

## No selling, no sharing

The app collects none of what you log, so there is nothing to sell or share. Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers or data brokers, or used for advertising.

## How long data is kept

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. ApolloF keeps nothing you log, because nothing is received.

## Deleting your data

- Delete single entries in the app, or clear everything with *Android Settings → Apps → (this app) → Storage → Clear storage*.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all its data from the phone.
- Backup and report files you exported are yours to delete where you saved them.
- Your Pro purchase stays linked to your Google account; Google Play restores it when you install the app again.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data about you, restrict or object to its use, and get a copy of it. The only personal data ApolloF may hold is an email you send and the Google Play sales reports described above; an email is used only to answer you and deleted when it is no longer needed. Write to me@apollof.nl. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/), or the authority where you live.

## Children

The app is meant for adults (18+) and isn't directed at children. It doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version is published with a new date at the top.
